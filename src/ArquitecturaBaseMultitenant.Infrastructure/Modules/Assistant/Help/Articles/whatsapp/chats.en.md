# WhatsApp chats

Chats has two inboxes: Customers and Internal, where employees writing from their verified phone land. You see each inbox if you have its permission. Taking a chat assigns it to you; if someone else took it first, your message is not sent. You can transfer it to a team or a person and leave notes, which the customer never sees. After 24 hours from the customer's last message, you can only write with an approved template.
